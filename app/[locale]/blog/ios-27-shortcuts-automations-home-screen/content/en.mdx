# iOS 27 Shortcuts Automations That Improve Your Home Screen

**Quick answer:** open Shortcuts, tap **Automation**, then the plus button, and pick a trigger. The interesting new one on iOS 27 fires when a specific app posts a notification, and it can run silently. Below are five automations that change what your Home Screen does, rather than five that just look clever.

---

## 1. Notification dots on a launcher widget

A widget cannot read your unread counts; Apple does not expose them. But an automation can notice that an app posted a notification, and a widget can read what the automation wrote.

That is exactly how [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) puts a dot on a launcher tile. You pick the apps, the automation runs silently when one of them notifies you, and the tile gets a dot until you open the app. There is a full walkthrough in [notification badges on widgets](/blog/notification-badges-on-widgets-iphone).

Two settings matter when you build the automation: **Run Immediately** on, **Notify When Run** off. Otherwise every notification produces a second notification, which is a special kind of annoying.

## 2. A focus page that follows the time

Set an automation for your working hours that turns on a Focus, and pair the Focus with a Home Screen page. iOS switches pages with the Focus, so at nine in the morning the iPhone shows your work page and at six it shows the other one.

This works better than rearranging icons by hand, and it is the one automation almost everybody keeps.

## 3. Arriving somewhere opens the thing you always open

Trigger: **Arrive**, at the gym, the office, a shop you visit weekly. Action: open the app you always open there, or start a playlist.

Keep it to places you genuinely repeat. An automation that fires somewhere you go twice a year is noise you will have to debug later.

## 4. Plugging in at night

Trigger: **Charger** connected, with a time condition. Actions: Low Power Mode off, alarm check, Focus on. On iPadOS and iOS both, this is the cheapest automation to set up and the one you notice most, because it removes a nightly chore.

## 5. A single tile that runs a whole routine

A Shortcut does not need to live in the Shortcuts app. WidgetLoft tiles can run Shortcuts, so a launcher widget can hold "leave for work" or "start recording" next to your apps. One grid, apps and routines together, no separate Shortcuts widget taking up space.

## What to watch out for

- **Test before you trust.** Trigger the condition once yourself and look at the result. Automations fail quietly.
- **Check the toggles.** New automations default to asking before they run, and the notification trigger defaults to Automation on and Notify off. Both are worth confirming.
- **Keep them few.** Ten automations you forgot about behave like a haunted phone. Five that you can name is a system.

## Common questions

**What is the notification trigger?**
An automation that fires when a chosen app posts a notification. It arrived with iOS 27 and can run without showing anything.

**Do automations run without asking me?**
Yes, when Run Immediately is on. Turn Notify When Run off too for a silent one.

**Can an automation change what a widget shows?**
It can change what a widget reads. The notification dots on WidgetLoft tiles work this way.
