# Clear, Tinted and Dark App Icons in iOS 27 (and the Glass Slider)

**Quick answer:** long-press an empty spot on the Home Screen, tap **Edit**, then **Customise**, and pick Light, Dark, Clear or Tinted for your icons. For the rest of the interface, open **Settings, Appearance, Liquid Glass** and drag the slider. Two different controls, two different jobs, and people mix them up constantly.

---

## The two controls, separated

**Icon appearance** lives on the Home Screen, not in Settings. It changes how app icons are drawn: normal light icons, dark versions, a glassy Clear look, or Tinted where you pick a colour and every icon adopts it.

**The Liquid Glass slider** lives in Settings, Appearance, Liquid Glass, and it is new in iOS 27. It does not touch your icons. It controls how transparent the system interface is: menus, toolbars, sheets, Control Centre. Pull it toward Clear and everything reads lighter and more see-through; pull it toward Tinted and the same surfaces gain a solid backing, which is easier to read against a busy wallpaper.

There is no switch that turns Liquid Glass off. The slider is the control Apple gave, and dragging it to Tinted is as close as you get.

## How to change icon appearance

1. Long-press an empty part of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
2. Tap **Edit**, then **Customise**.
3. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, **Clear** or **Tinted**. Tinted gives you a colour picker.
4. Tap anywhere to finish.

Clear is the one that gets attention: icons become glassy and take colour from the wallpaper. It looks best over a wallpaper with some contrast and worst over a busy photo, where several icons stop being distinguishable.

## The part nobody mentions: widgets do not follow

Icon appearance is applied by the system to app icons. A widget draws its own content, so it does not inherit Clear or Tinted. Set your Home Screen to Dark and a widget full of bright app icons suddenly looks like a hole in the page.

- **Per-appearance custom icons.** You can set one image for light mode and a different one for dark, per app.

## Which combination actually looks good

- **Clear icons, a plain wallpaper, transparent widgets.** The cleanest look iOS 27 can produce, and the one the glass effect was designed for.
- **Dark icons, dark wallpaper, dark widget tiles.** The most legible, and the least dependent on your wallpaper.
- **Tinted icons in one colour, matching custom widget tiles.** The most personal, and the most work.

Avoid Tinted with a photo wallpaper. Everything turns into the same coloured mush and you start hunting for apps by position.

## Common questions

**Can I turn Liquid Glass off in iOS 27?**
No. The slider in Settings, Appearance, Liquid Glass is the only control, and it moves between clearer and more tinted.

**Does Clear apply to every app?**
It applies to the Home Screen as a whole. Apps that ship proper icon layers look right; the rest are approximated.

**Do widget app tiles follow the icon appearance?**
No, a widget draws itself. That is why WidgetLoft ships real dark icons and per-appearance overrides.
