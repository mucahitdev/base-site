# The Fastest Ways to Open Apps on iPhone in iOS 27 (Siri AI, Widgets and More)

**Quick answer:** Match the method to how often you use the app. Your number one app goes on the **Action button**. Your top 5 go in the **Dynamic Island** with a Quick Launch bar. Your top 20 go in **one launcher widget** on your first Home Screen page, one tap each. Everything else is a **Siri AI** request or a **Spotlight** search away.

iOS 27 added a much smarter Siri, but talking to your phone is not always the fastest way to open Instagram on a train. Here is how the options compare.

---

## Seven ways to open an app in iOS 27

**1. Siri AI.** Hold the side button and say "open Maps", or type it in the new Siri app. Siri AI needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer. It launched in English, and the iOS 27.2 beta adds French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Spanish. Great for apps you rarely open, slower than a tap for daily ones.

**2. Spotlight.** Swipe down on the Home Screen, type two or three letters, tap the result. Fast and private, but it needs both hands most of the time.

**3. App Library.** Swipe past your last Home Screen page. Good for browsing, but it takes a swipe or two, plus a search or a scroll.

**4. Action button.** On iPhones that have one, set it to open a single app from anywhere, even from inside another app. Only one app, though.

**5. Home Screen icons.** One tap, but only for apps on the page you are on. Every extra page costs a swipe.

**6. A launcher widget.** A [WidgetLoft](/widgetloft) widget puts many apps on one page: 9 in a Small widget, 21 in a Medium, 49 in a Large and up to 77 in the iOS 27 Extra Large size, with small icons. A tap opens the app directly, and sub-folders hold even more behind one tile.

**7. The Dynamic Island.** With WidgetLoft's Quick Launch bar, long-press the Dynamic Island and up to 5 apps appear. The same bar shows on the Lock Screen with up to 10 apps. More in [how to put apps in the Dynamic Island](/blog/apps-in-dynamic-island-iphone).

## How they compare

| Method | Steps to open | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action button | 1 press | Your single most-used app |
| Home Screen widget tile | 1 tap | Your top 10 to 20 apps |
| Dynamic Island Quick Launch | Long press + 1 tap | Your top 5, from inside other apps |
| Lock Screen widget | 1 tap + unlock | Up to 4 apps before you unlock |
| Spotlight | Swipe + type + tap | Apps you know by name |
| Siri AI | Hold + speak | Apps you rarely open, hands busy |
| App Library | Swipe + search or scroll | Browsing everything you have |

## Step-by-step: set up a fast layout

1. **Sort your apps.** Write down your top 1, top 5 and top 20.
2. **Top 1 → Action button.** Open **Settings → Action Button** and set it to open the app.
3. **Top 5 → Dynamic Island.** In WidgetLoft, go to **Settings → Dynamic Island & Live Activity**, turn on **Quick Launch bar** and pick a folder.
4. **Top 20 → one widget.** Make a WidgetLoft group and add it as a Medium or Large widget on page one. Want a whole page? See [the iOS 27 Extra Large widget](/blog/extra-large-widget-ios-27).
5. **Everything else → Siri AI or Spotlight.** No icons needed for apps you open once a month.

## Common questions

**Can Siri AI open apps?**
Yes. Hold the side button and ask, or type in the Siri app. Siri AI needs an iPhone 15 Pro or newer.

**What is the fastest way to open an app on iPhone?**
For apps you use often, a one-tap tile on your current Home Screen page. For one single app, the Action button, because it works from anywhere.

**Do widget tiles open the app directly?**
With WidgetLoft, yes: no Shortcuts app and no WidgetLoft screen in between. From the Lock Screen, iOS asks you to unlock first.
